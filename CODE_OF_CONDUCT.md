# Code of conduct

Luach is a project about the Jewish calendar, built by and for people who care about getting it right. We ask everyone
who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented) to:

- **Be respectful.** Communities keep different customs. When opinions differ, say what yours is based on and accept
  that others follow theirs. Luach records customs; it doesn't rank them.
- **Be kind and patient,** especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language.
- **Assume good faith,** and discuss the work, not the person.
- **Keep it on topic.** This isn't the place for politics or for debating anyone's level of observance.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, sharing someone's private information,
or sexual language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the repository's Security tab, "Report a vulnerability",
works for conduct reports too). The maintainer will review every report, keep it confidential, and may remove
contributions or block participants who break this code.
