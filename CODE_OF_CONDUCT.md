# Code of conduct

Luach is built for people who keep the Jewish calendar, in every community. Everyone who
takes part here (in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) is expected to:

- be courteous and patient, especially with newcomers and with people whose first language
  is not English;
- discuss code, data and sources, not people;
- respect every community's customs: when minhagim differ, the library offers each one and
  decides nothing, and discussions here should do the same;
- leave out personal attacks, harassment, insults, and sexual or demeaning content;
- keep other people's private information private.

Halachic questions belong with a rav. Here we discuss what a source says and how to
calculate it, not what anyone should do.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block anyone who does not
follow this code. To report a problem privately, use the contact details on the
maintainer's GitHub profile ([@yskolnick](https://github.com/yskolnick)). Reports are kept
confidential.
