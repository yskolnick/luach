# Licence and how to credit

Luach is free software under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). You may use it in
any project, including commercial apps.

## What the licence requires

If you redistribute Luach, including inside an app bundle, keep the text of its `NOTICE` file with it, for example
on your app's open-source licences screen. The NOTICE reads, in part:

> Built on Luach by Yosef Skolnick.

It also credits GeoNames (CC-BY 4.0) for the city data.

## What we ask

The licence can't require it, but we ask that apps show the line above somewhere users can see it, such as an About
or Credits screen.

## Data

Every dataset Luach ships and its licence is listed in [Data sources](/data-sources).
