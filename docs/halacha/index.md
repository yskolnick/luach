# Halachic notes

> **Ask your rav. This library calculates; it does not rule.**

Luach offers opinions; you choose one. Every zman names whose opinion it follows and where
it is written; every custom that commonly differs is an option; and every default is documented
as a default, never as "the" correct answer.

- [Zmanim: every opinion and its source](./zmanim)
- [Halachic choices and calculations](./choices): every place Luach chose between opinions,
  customs or ways to calculate, with its sources, its confidence, and what is open.
- The calendar follows the Rambam, Hilchos Kiddush HaChodesh 6–8 (molad, the 19-year cycle,
  the four dechiyos, year types).
- Holidays and fasts cite the Shulchan Aruch where they are moved (SA OC 549–550, 552:10,
  686:2, 470:2…); see the source of `src/holidays/holidays.ts`.
- Yahrzeits and birthdays in Adar and on the 30th of Cheshvan/Kislev: both customs, with
  sources, on `anniversary`.
- Haftaros by minhag and their precedence: `haftaraFor`, and the research note on haftaros.
- Citations were checked against the texts:
  [M1–M2 check](https://github.com/yskolnick/luach/blob/main/docs/research/2026-10-21-citation-check-m1-m2.md),
  [zmanim](https://github.com/yskolnick/luach/blob/main/docs/research/2026-10-21-zmanim-opinions.md).
