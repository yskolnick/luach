# Zmanim: opinions and sources

> Ask your rav. This library calculates; it does not rule.

Every zman Luach computes, generated from `src/zmanim/opinions.ts`. "Rounding" is how a
displayed time is rounded to the minute: earliest times up, deadlines down. Citations were
checked against the texts (see `docs/research/2026-10-21-zmanim-opinions.md`); where an
attribution could not be checked, the source says "not verified".
